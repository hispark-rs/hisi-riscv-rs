Timer / Watchdog counting clock (= TCXO_HZ, 24 MHz).

Use this — not SYSTEM_CLOCK_HZ — to convert microseconds/milliseconds to Timer or WDT counter ticks. The timer is clocked by the crystal, so at 24 MHz one microsecond is 24 ticks (the vendor timer_porting_us_2_cycle computes us * (clock / 1_000_000) = us * 24).
