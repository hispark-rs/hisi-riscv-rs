# WS63 RF A1 Crypto Provider Migration - 2026-07-13

## Scope

This evidence records extraction of `hisi-crypto 0.1.0-alpha.2` and migration
of the WPA2-Personal path. It proves the provider boundary and current WS63
backend; it does not stabilize SPACC or claim WPA3/TLS support.

## Boundary

- `hisi-crypto` owns the chip-neutral provider contract and portable PBKDF2,
  SHA-1/SHA-256, HMAC-SHA1/HMAC-SHA256 and AES block implementation.
- The RustCrypto backend is the software implementation and known-answer-test
  oracle. Its entropy method deliberately returns `Unsupported`.
- The WS63 RF adapter owns unified-cipher PBKDF2/TRNG calls and the temporary C
  supplicant ABI. SHA/HMAC/AES delegate through `hisi-crypto`.
- `ws63-rf-rs` no longer directly depends on the five RustCrypto algorithm
  crates. Passwords and key material remain outside public RF types and logs.

## Silicon Parity

The canonical image was transferred at 115200 baud with explicit loaderboot,
then started with a physical J-Link reset. The WPA2 credential was injected
through the environment and is absent from this evidence.

```text
RF1_IMAGE_OK
RF2_INIT_BEGIN
RF5B_WPA_CONNECT_OK freq=0x0000096c
RF5A_DHCP_OK addr=192.168.155.2 prefix=0x00000018 router=192.168.155.1
RF5A_ARP_OK rx=0x00000004
RF5C_PING_OK rx=0x00000005
```

The changed body hash and successful final marker prove that the new crypto
dependency graph, rather than the earlier pre-extraction image, ran on silicon.
